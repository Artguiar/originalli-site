## Brazilian illustrative replacements

User-requested executive Brazilian consultation and Brazilian family scenes were generated with imagegen, 1536×1024, then encoded as WebP for the website. They depict fictional people and are not photos of actual Originalli clients or offices.
- assets/consultoria-executiva-brasil.webp: professional executive meeting in a contemporary Brazilian office, natural daylight.
- assets/familia-brasileira.webp: parents and two children interacting in a contemporary Brazilian home, lightweight summer clothing, natural daylight.
These replace the consultation/family stock-photo references below on the homepage and People page.

Images are illustrative, not representations of Originalli employees or clients.

## Integration boundaries

Quiver and insurance price-calculation APIs remain unconfigured. Existing client login is preserved. Quote requests are explicitly described as WhatsApp-assisted, not instant pricing.

FIPE uses the documented unauthenticated independent API https://deividfortuna.github.io/fipe/ with provider limits and an official-source fallback. No API secrets are embedded.

BO direct links verified for PR and SP. Other selections explicitly refer to the federal portal for availability; not represented as direct state integration. Reference: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/registrar-ocorrencia-policial-online

Temperature not implemented: the prototype's Open-Meteo free endpoint needs commercial-use licensing review for this business website. No simulated weather is displayed.
